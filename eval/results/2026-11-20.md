**Source:** `gold.agg_pacing_fee_line`, one row per case-rate fee line. Per `AGENTS.md`, I queried only this table and `rpt_data_quality_register`, with no joins.

## SQL

```sql
-- 1. Every case-rate fee line, worst pacing first
select fee_id, employer_name, program_name, effective_start, effective_end, go_live_date,
       elapsed_days, round(elapsed_share,3) as elapsed_share, projected_episodes,
       round(expected_episodes,2) as expected_episodes, completed_episodes,
       round(shortfall_episodes,2) as shortfall_episodes, round(pacing_ratio,3) as pacing_ratio,
       rate_usd, discount_rate, revenue_at_risk_usd,
       is_window_closed, is_go_live_after_window_start, is_unattributed, client_success_action
from gold.agg_pacing_fee_line
order by pacing_ratio asc nulls last, revenue_at_risk_usd desc;

-- 2. Totals per employer (the ratio is recomputed from sums because it can't be added up)
select employer_name, count(*) as fee_lines,
       sum(completed_episodes) as completed, round(sum(expected_episodes),2) as expected,
       round(sum(completed_episodes)/sum(expected_episodes),3) as pacing_ratio,
       sum(revenue_at_risk_usd) as revenue_at_risk_usd,
       string_agg(fee_id || ':' || coalesce(client_success_action,'on pace'), ', ' order by fee_id) as lines
from gold.agg_pacing_fee_line
group by employer_name order by revenue_at_risk_usd desc;

-- 3. Caveats
select * from gold.rpt_data_quality_register;
```

How the columns are calculated:
- **Expected** = projected episodes × the share of the fee line's window that has passed. Most windows are calendar 2026, so 181 of 365 days have passed (0.496).
- **Pacing** = completed ÷ expected.
- **Revenue at risk** = shortfall in episodes × case rate × (1 − discount). This is the revenue lost if the shortfall is never made up.

The other six lines are on pace (0.82 to 1.14).

## Why the ranking looks the way it does

- **The pacing ratio measures the gap in percentage terms; revenue at risk measures it in dollars.** FEE-307 is worst by ratio (0.25), but it only expected about 20 episodes. FEE-304 is almost on the 0.75 threshold (0.739), but it is a 300-episode line. A 26% gap on that volume at $3,000 per episode is the largest dollar exposure: $116k. Ranking by dollars puts FEE-304 first and FEE-307 second.
- **Lines with large shortfalls but no escalation are excluded by the model's rule.** `client_success_action` is the repo's documented rule. A line is escalated only if all of these hold: pacing below 0.75, at least $40k at risk, at least 60 days elapsed, an attributed employer, and no go-live gap.
  - FEE-311 is excluded because of the go-live gap.
  - FEE-316 is excluded because only 30 days have elapsed and its risk is under $40k.
  - FEE-310 is excluded because its risk is under $40k.
