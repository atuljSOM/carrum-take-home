**Question.** Return the case-rate fee lines pacing worst against projection as of 2026-06-30. For each, show completed episodes, expected episodes, pacing ratio, and the revenue at risk if the shortfall is never made up. Tell us which ones you would actually escalate to Client Success, and why the ranking is what it is.

**Expected.** FEE-307, 0.2521, FEE-303, FEE-304, FEE-305, FEE-307, FEE-312

**Answer.**

As of 2026-06-30, 8 of the 14 case-rate fee lines are pacing below 0.75 of projection. I'd escalate 5 of them to Client Success, covering 3 employers. The other 3 should be watched; for two of them the shortfall comes mostly from the data rather than from patients not getting care.

All numbers come from `gold.agg_pacing_fee_line` (one row per case-rate fee line, as of 2026-06-30), which is the table `AGENTS.md` names for pacing and escalation questions.

## SQL

```sql
-- 1. Lines pacing below 0.75
select fee_id, employer_name, program_name, effective_start, effective_end, go_live_date,
       elapsed_days, window_days, projected_episodes,
       round(expected_episodes,1) expected, completed_episodes,
       round(pacing_ratio,3) pacing, rate_usd, discount_rate,
       round(revenue_at_risk_usd,0) at_risk,
       is_window_closed, is_go_live_after_window_start, is_unattributed, client_success_action
from gold.agg_pacing_fee_line
where pacing_ratio < 0.75
order by pacing_ratio, revenue_at_risk_usd desc;

-- 2. Escalated lines rolled up by employer (ratio recomputed from sums, because it doesn't add up across rows)
select employer_name, count(*) lines, sum(completed_episodes) completed,
       round(sum(expected_episodes),1) expected,
       round(sum(completed_episodes)/sum(expected_episodes),3) pacing,
       round(sum(revenue_at_risk_usd),0) at_risk
from gold.agg_pacing_fee_line
where client_success_action = 'escalate'
group by employer_name order by at_risk desc;

-- 3. Data-quality caveats
select * from gold.rpt_data_quality_register;
```

How the columns are calculated:
- **Expected:** projected episodes × the share of the fee line's contract window that has passed by 2026-06-30.
- **Revenue at risk:** the shortfall (expected − completed, never below 0) × case rate × (1 − discount). This is revenue lost to date if the shortfall is never made up. It is not a full-year forecast.

## Why the ranking is what it is

Sorting by pacing ratio and sorting by dollars give different orders. FEE-304 has the second-best ratio on the list but the most dollars at risk. FEE-307 has the worst ratio but is a small line. I ranked escalations by dollars at risk grouped by employer, because Client Success talks to employers, not to individual fee lines.

The escalate/watch label comes from the `client_success_action` column. It is a documented judgement call, not a contract term. A line is marked "escalate" when all of these hold:
- pacing is below 0.75
- revenue at risk is at least $40K
- at least 60 days of the window have passed
- the line has a known employer
- the program went live on time

- **FEE-316 (Riverbend MSK):** this is probably a data problem, not low use. Four completed episodes had surgery before the line's 2026-06-01 start date, so they are excluded from this line. Counting them, pacing would be 1.25 (DQ-08). Only 30 days have passed, and $11K is at risk. The contract dates or fee assignment should be fixed upstream.
- **FEE-310 (Riverbend Bariatric):** it is only slightly behind (0.73), with $14.7K at risk.

## Caveats

- **Possible missing completions (DQ-22):** six episodes are still marked SCHEDULED even though their surgery date has passed, and all six show as COMPLETED in records dated after 2026-06-30. The approved table doesn't say which fee lines they're on, so I can't tell whether they affect any escalated line. It would be worth checking before the Bayview conversation.
- **What this doesn't cover:** the table can't say *why* use is low (for example eligibility, member engagement or provider access), and it has no forecasts beyond 2026-06-30.
